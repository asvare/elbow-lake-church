Our Story

About Elbow Lake Lutheran Church

A congregation rooted in faith, tradition, and love for our neighbors in Waubun, Minnesota.

Our History

Faithful to Our Community for Generations

Elbow Lake Lutheran Church has served the Waubun community for generations, a steady place of worship and fellowship for all who walk through its doors.

We carry a rich Lutheran heritage, shaped by the faithful people who have worshipped here across the years.

We gather each Sunday for the Word, the sacraments, and one another. All are welcome.

"Love one another. As I have loved you, so you must love one another."

Our Faith

What We Believe

The Gospel shows the way you can be saved through a personal relationship with God.

Everyone is a sinner.

"All have sinned, and come short of the glory of God." — Romans 3:23

Death is the consequence for sin.

"The wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord." — Romans 6:23

God loves everyone.

"For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life." — John 3:16

Jesus died because of our sins.

"God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us." — Romans 5:8

Jesus rose again from the dead.

"Christ died for our sins according to the scriptures; and that he was buried, and that he rose again the third day according to the scriptures." — 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

Repent and call on Jesus to save you.

"For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved." — Romans 10:13
