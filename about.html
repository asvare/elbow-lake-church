Our Story

About Elbow Lake Lutheran Church

A congregation rooted in faith, tradition, and love for our neighbors in Waubun, Minnesota.

Our History

Faithful to Our Community for Generations

Elbow Lake Lutheran Church has served the Waubun community for many years, standing as a place of worship, fellowship, and refuge for all who come through its doors.

Our congregation carries a rich heritage of Lutheran faith — shaped by the grace of God, the community around Elbow Lake, and the faithful people who have worshipped here across the years.

We gather each Sunday to hear the Word preached, receive the sacraments, and encourage one another in the life of faith. Our doors are open to all.

Our Faith

What We Believe

We are a Lutheran congregation grounded in the historic Christian faith, the authority of Scripture, and the grace of God revealed in Jesus Christ.

📖

Scripture Alone

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the authoritative guide for faith and life. All teaching is measured against Scripture.

✚

Grace Alone

Salvation is a free gift of God's grace through Jesus Christ — not earned by works, but received through faith. This is the heart of the Lutheran message.

🙏

Faith Alone

We are justified before God through faith in Jesus Christ alone. This faith is a gift of the Holy Spirit, working through Word and Sacrament.

🏭

The Sacraments

We celebrate Holy Baptism and the Lord's Supper as means of grace instituted by Christ, through which God truly works in the lives of believers.

👥

Community & Fellowship

We are one body in Christ, called to love and serve one another and our neighbors — bearing each other's burdens in the spirit of Christian love.

🌎

Service & Outreach

Faith is active in love. We are called to serve our community, care for those in need, and share the good news of Jesus Christ with those around us.

Our Mission

Why We Gather

Our mission is simple: to know God, to grow in faith together, and to make that love known to the world around us.

We worship, we study, we pray, and we serve — not to earn God's favor, but as a grateful response to the grace we have already received.

Gather

We come together each Sunday for worship, Word, and Sacrament.

Grow

Through Scripture study, prayer, and fellowship in the faith.

Give

Offering our time, talents, and resources for God's work.

Go

Carrying the love of Christ into our community and beyond.
