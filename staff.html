Senior Pastor

Pastor Mark Kiser

Pastor Mark Kiser comes from a long line of Christian ministry, with parents who served over 40 years as missionaries in Mexico. He has ministered in the U.S., Mexico, and Australia, working in Christian education since 1977 and pastoring a church on a Minnesota Native American reservation for 16 years.

Pastor Kiser holds multiple degrees in theology, biblical studies, leadership, and Christian education, and is currently pursuing a doctorate. Each Sunday, his message of God's grace is proclaimed with clarity and conviction — in the sanctuary and through Facebook Live, reaching members and friends wherever they may be.
