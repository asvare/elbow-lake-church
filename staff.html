Our Pastor

Pastor Mark Kiser — serving Elbow Lake Lutheran Church with decades of ministry experience and a deep love for this community.

Senior Pastor

Pastor Mark Kiser

Pastor Mark Kiser comes from a family of ministry, with parents who served over 40 years as missionaries in Mexico. He has ministered in the U.S., Mexico, and Australia, in Christian education since 1977, and pastored on a Minnesota Native American reservation for 16 years.

He holds degrees in theology, biblical studies, leadership, and Christian education, and is currently pursuing a doctorate. Every Sunday his message of grace reaches the congregation in person and live on Facebook.
