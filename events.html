Brad Brandon
Founder & CEO, Across Nigeria

Brad Brandon spent over twenty years in pastoral ministry and a decade as a daily drive-time radio talk show host for Salem Communications. His writing has appeared in the Daily Wire, Washington Times, Townhall, and The Christian Post, and he is a regular contributor on Fox News, Newsmax, Glenn Beck, and Washington Watch. Since 2018, he has served on foreign mission fields in high-risk, highly persecuted regions, primarily in northern Nigeria and West Africa. Through Across Nigeria, Pastor Brad and his team are bringing education and literacy training to Muslim communities while providing aid and relief to Christian communities facing intense persecution.
