Sunday Worship — Guest Speaker Brad Brandon
9:00 AM — Father's Day Sunday
Pastor Brad Brandon, founder of Across Nigeria, joins us to share about his mission work in northern Nigeria and West Africa. Don't miss this powerful morning of worship and testimony.
Brad Brandon
Founder & CEO, Across Nigeria

Brad Brandon spent over twenty years in pastoral ministry and a decade as a daily drive-time radio talk show host for Salem Communications. His writing has appeared in the Daily Wire, Washington Times, Townhall, and The Christian Post, and he is a regular contributor on Fox News, Newsmax, Glenn Beck, and Washington Watch. Since 2018, he has served on foreign mission fields in high-risk, highly persecuted regions, primarily in northern Nigeria and West Africa. Through Across Nigeria, Pastor Brad and his team are bringing education and literacy training to Muslim communities while providing aid and relief to Christian communities facing intense persecution.

Michael & Luda Smyshliaieve
Eastern Ukraine — Kherson Mission

Michael and Luda are from eastern Ukraine. Luda's father pastors the mission the church supports in Kherson. Their congregation provides vital community services including fresh water, shelter, meals, and ministries to every age group in one of the most affected regions of the country.

Ivan & Inna
Western Ukraine — Front Lines Ministry

Ivan and Inna are from western Ukraine, a region well known for sending soldiers and aid to the front lines in the war with Russia. Their church participates in several active ministries throughout the region, supporting both those serving in the military and the communities impacted by the conflict.

Drs. Gary & Lynn Fuller
Founders, Gentle Shepherd Baptist Church

Drs. Gary and Lynn Fuller have been in ministry for decades, serving through preaching, teaching, music, missions, and much more. They are the founders of Gentle Shepherd Baptist Church, which recently celebrated its 29th anniversary. Lynn serves as the principal of a large elementary school in Lincoln and is an accomplished public speaker. Together, Gary and Lynn bring a lifetime of faith, service, and dedication to every congregation they visit.
