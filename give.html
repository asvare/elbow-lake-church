Faithful Giving

Give to the Church

Your generosity sustains the ministry, blesses our community, and furthers the work of God's kingdom.

Why We Give

Giving Is an Act of Worship

Giving is a joyful response to everything God has given us. Every gift supports the ongoing work of ministry, worship, and care for our neighbors.

Your giving maintains our building, supports our Pastor, and funds outreach in our community.

Worship & Ministry

Supporting weekly worship services, Sacraments, and pastoral care.

Church Maintenance

Keeping our building and grounds ready to welcome all who come.

Community Outreach

Reaching out to neighbors in need and sharing the love of Christ.

Faith Formation

Supporting Bible study, fellowship, and growth in the faith.

"Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver."
2 Corinthians 9:7
