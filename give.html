Faithful Giving

Give to the Church

Your generosity sustains the ministry, blesses our community, and furthers the work of God's kingdom.

Why We Give

Giving Is an Act of Worship

The Scriptures teach that giving is not a burden but a blessing — a joyful, grateful response to everything God has given us. Every gift to Elbow Lake Lutheran Church, no matter the size, supports the ongoing work of ministry, worship, and care for our neighbors.

Your contribution helps maintain our church building, support our Pastor, fund community outreach, and keep our doors open for all who come.

Worship & Ministry

Supporting weekly worship services, Sacraments, and pastoral care.

Church Maintenance

Keeping our building and grounds ready to welcome all who come.

Community Outreach

Reaching out to neighbors in need and sharing the love of Christ.

Faith Formation

Supporting Bible study, fellowship, and growth in the faith.
