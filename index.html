Our Community

A Place of Faith, Fellowship & Welcome

Elbow Lake Lutheran Church has been a cornerstone of faith in the Waubun community for generations. We are a congregation rooted in Lutheran tradition, gathering together each week to worship, study scripture, and care for one another.

Whether you are a lifelong Lutheran, new to the faith, or simply looking for a community — you are welcome here.

Worship

Gathering each Sunday to praise God through Word and Sacrament.

Community

Caring for one another and our neighbors in the spirit of Christ.

Scripture

Grounded in God's Word as the foundation of our faith and life.

Service

Serving others as an expression of our love for God and neighbor.

Our Pastor

Guided by Faithful Leadership

Pastor Mark Kiser brings decades of ministry experience across the U.S., Mexico, and Australia — including 16 years pastoring on a Minnesota Native American reservation. Each week his message of God's grace is delivered with care and conviction, in person and through Facebook Live.
